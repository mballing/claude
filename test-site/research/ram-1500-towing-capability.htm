How Much Can the 2020 Ram 1500 Tow?

Published: March 15, 2020  |  Category: Research & Reviews

If you're shopping for a half-ton pickup truck and towing capacity is near the top of your priority list, the 2020 Ram 1500 is one of the most impressive options in its class. With the right configuration, this truck can tow up to 12,750 lbs — a figure that edges out most of its competitors.

2020 Ram 1500 Maximum Towing Capacity by Engine

Towing capacity varies significantly depending on which powertrain you choose. Here's a breakdown of every available engine and its maximum tow rating:

Engine Displacement Horsepower Torque Max Towing
3.6L Pentastar V6 3.6L 305 hp 269 lb-ft 7,730 lbs
5.7L HEMI V8 5.7L 395 hp 410 lb-ft 11,610 lbs
5.7L HEMI V8 + eTorque 5.7L 395 hp 410 lb-ft 12,750 lbs
3.0L EcoDiesel V6 3.0L 260 hp 480 lb-ft 12,560 lbs

What Can You Actually Tow at 12,750 Lbs?

At maximum capacity, the Ram 1500 can handle a wide range of trailers and loads:

  • A fully-loaded 30-ft travel trailer (typical weight: 8,000–12,000 lbs)
  • A mid-size boat and trailer combo (up to 10,000 lbs)
  • Two or three ATVs on a car hauler trailer
  • A horse trailer with two horses and gear
  • A small equipment trailer with a skid steer or mini-excavator

The 5.7L HEMI V8 with eTorque is the secret weapon behind the Ram 1500's class-leading tow rating. The mild-hybrid system adds 90 lb-ft of instantaneous electric torque at low RPM — exactly when you need it most during initial acceleration with a heavy trailer in tow.

Required Packages for Maximum Towing

To achieve the 12,750-lb rating you'll need the following equipment on your Ram 1500:

  • Max Tow Package — upgrades the rear axle ratio, adds a trailer brake controller, and includes a Class IV receiver hitch
  • 5.7L HEMI V8 with eTorque — required engine for this rating
  • Crew Cab or Quad Cab body style — Regular Cab is limited to 8,800 lbs
  • 4×2 drivetrain — 4×4 models are rated slightly lower at 12,250 lbs

Comparing the Ram 1500 to the Competition

Truck Max Towing (2020) Required Engine

Ford F-150 13,200 lbs 3.5L EcoBoost V6
Chevrolet Silverado 1500 13,400 lbs 6.2L V8
GMC Sierra 1500 13,400 lbs 6.2L V8
Toyota Tundra 10,200 lbs 5.7L V8

Trailer Hitch & Receiver Options

The Ram 1500 comes standard with a 2-inch square receiver hitch on most trims. When equipped with the Max Tow Package, the factory Class IV hitch supports up to 12,750 lbs of gross trailer weight and 1,000 lbs of tongue weight. The built-in trailer brake controller is factory-integrated into the instrument cluster for easy adjustment.

Final Verdict

The 2020 Ram 1500 is a serious towing truck, especially with the HEMI V8 and eTorque option. While it falls slightly behind the F-150 and Silverado at the top of the chart, it leads in ride quality, interior refinement, and the unique coil-spring rear suspension that makes it more comfortable on the road with or without a trailer attached.
